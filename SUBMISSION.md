## 2. One-Line & Short Pitch

### One-Line Pitch
Your wallet is a troll level. The chain already trolled you — survive it this time.

### Short Description
RELIC is an interactive on-chain archaeologist that transforms a wallet's real history into a custom 3D voxel platformer. By analyzing your genesis date, DeFi swaps, NFT mints, failed transactions, gas burned, and market drawdown endurance, RELIC constructs a tailored level where every trap is receipts-backed (e.g., your longest dormancy is a long flat stretch of boredom, a failed transaction is a fake exit, and selling an asset too early is a coin that runs away from you). 

Survive your own history, receive a deterministic Kaizenverse character sheet (incorporating one of 6 unique archetypes, a 5-bar stat block, and evidence-backed personality notes), and mint your character on Mantle Sepolia. As your wallet continues to live on-chain, your NFT can evolve under the "kaizen" (continuous improvement) mechanic.
